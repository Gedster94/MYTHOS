Privacy Policy — The Guiding Light

What the game stores

Your game progress (answers, deeds, Shards, artifacts, themes), your rank score, your username and the artifacts you show off. If you create an account, your email address and password are stored too. The password is kept scrambled by our login provider, and the game never sees it.

Guests

If you play without an account, you get an anonymous ID so your progress can be saved. No name or email is needed.

What other players can see

Your username, your rank score and your showcased artifacts appear on leaderboards. In online modes (Duel, Rival, Party, Vault and Friends), the players in the match can see your username and score. Nobody can see your email.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database service. The game page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Sign-up emails are sent through Gmail. The game has no ads, no analytics trackers, and your data is never sold.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from the Profile screen. This removes your progress, your username, your friends list and your online match history, and it cannot be undone.

Age

MYTHOS is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 13, please don't create an account or share personal information.

Reports, blocks and invites

If you report or block another player, or use an invite link, the game stores that link between the two accounts so it can work (for example, to keep a blocked player away from you, or to pay an invite bonus). Reports are read by the game's owner. All of it is deleted with your account.

Questions

If you have any questions about your data, or want help deleting it, write to mythosgame.noreply@gmail.com.

How to delete your account

Open the game, go to Profile, and choose Delete account. This removes your account and its data. If you can't use the app, write to mythosgame.noreply@gmail.com from the email you signed up with and we will delete it for you.
